Eye protection for your Mac — warm screen temperature and 20-20-20 eye rest reminders, running quietly from the menu bar.

System requirements

  • OS: macOS 12 (Monterey) or later recommended
  • Display warmth: Native gamma adjustment when supported by your display
  • Installer: DMG (130 MB)
  • Permissions: Notifications recommended for eye rest reminders

Native display warmth

SYE uses macOS system gamma for embedded display warmth when your hardware supports it — no overlay needed for most setups.
