What’s included in Version 1

  • Screen temperature — Day (5000K), Evening (3400K), Night (0K), manual slider
  • 20-20-20 eye rest with lecture mode, schedules, and quick start
  • Menu bar controls — toggle temperature, eye rest, break now, pause 30 min
  • Guided eye exercises, corner timers, battery saver, force stop

System requirements

  • Chip: Apple Silicon (M1, M2, M3, or later) — Intel Macs not supported
  • OS: macOS 12 (Monterey) or later recommended
  • Display warmth: Native display warmth when supported; overlay fallback otherwise
  • Installer: DMG (~107 MB) or ZIP (~98 MB) · signed & notarized
  • Permissions: Notifications recommended for eye rest reminders
