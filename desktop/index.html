Features

Everything your eyes need at the desk.

SYE helps reduce eye strain during long screen time with screen temperature and the 20-20-20 rule. Everything runs on your computer — no account, no cloud sync.

🌡️

Screen Temperature

Shift your display toward warmer tones with presets or a manual slider — less blue light at night.

  • Day — 5000K
  • Evening — 3400K
  • Night — 0K (deepest warm tone, zero blue light)
  • Slider from 0K (Night) to 6500K (cool / neutral)
  • Toggle on or off from the Temp tab or menu bar
  • Native display warmth on Mac when available; overlay fallback otherwise
  • Reset display clears SYE’s effect and returns normal macOS settings
👁️

Eye Rest (20-20-20)

Regular reminders to look away from the screen — default every 20 minutes, for 20 seconds.

  • Interval: 5 min – 2 hours · Break length: 5 sec – 10 min
  • Full-screen break overlay on all monitors
  • Reminders before each break (1 min and 10 sec always on; 5 min and 30 sec optional)
  • Lecture / reading mode — gentler copy for classes and reading
  • Skip button — optional; off by default
  • Break now and Pause 30 min from the menu bar
🧘

Guided Eye Exercises

Six on-demand routines with per-step timers. Longer exercises use an audio chime when each step changes.

  • 20-20-20 Focus — 20 sec
  • Figure Eight — ~1.5 min (audio)
  • Near–Far Focus — ~1.5 min (audio)
  • Blink Reset — ~45 sec (audio)
  • Palming Rest — 5 min (audio)
  • Distance Gaze — 5 min (audio)
⚡

Menu bar

SYE lives in the menu bar (not the Dock). Right-click the icon for quick actions.

  • Open SYE
  • Toggle screen temperature
  • Toggle eye rest
  • Break now · Pause eye rest 30 min
  • Quit
🏠

Quick start (Home)

One-tap modes for common situations. Pick a duration (up to 2 hours); SYE applies settings immediately and restores your previous setup when time is up.

  • Weekday classes — Day temperature + eye rest every 20 min
  • Late night cram — 0K night temperature + 30 min breaks + lecture mode
  • Active schedules always take priority over quick start
📅

Schedules

Automate temperature and eye rest by day and time.

  • Up to 5 schedules (e.g. school hours, late-night study)
  • When a schedule runs, it controls SYE until that window ends
  • Skip today or tomorrow per schedule
  • Schedules override manual changes and quick start while active
⏱️

Corner timers

Small draggable HUDs for time since last break or session length.

🕐

Active hours

Limit when SYE auto-starts on login (e.g. 9:00–18:00). You can still turn features on manually anytime.

🔋

Battery saver

Reduces background work and hides corner timers. On Mac, also follows macOS Low Power Mode.

🚀

Launch at login

Start SYE automatically when you sign in.

⏹️

Force stop

Instantly stops everything: skips all schedules for today, stops eye rest, turns off temperature, ends quick start.

How it works

Up and running in minutes.

1

Install

Download the DMG for Mac, open it, and drag SYE to Applications.

2

Enable temperature

Pick Day, Evening, or Night — or use the slider from the Temp tab.

3

Enable eye rest

Set your interval and break length, or try a quick start mode.

4

Keep working

SYE runs in the menu bar; reminders appear system-wide.
