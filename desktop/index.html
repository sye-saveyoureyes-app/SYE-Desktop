Features

Everything your eyes need at the desk.

Screen warmth, 20-20-20 breaks, and guided eye exercises — local-first, no account required, runs quietly in the background.

🌡️

Screen Temperature

Reduce blue light with embedded display warmth (native macOS gamma) or a transparent overlay fallback.

  • Day — 4400K / 70% brightness
  • Evening — 3400K / 80% brightness
  • Night — 0K / 100% (zero blue light, deep warm red)
  • Custom slider from 0K (warmest / Night) to 6500K (cool)
  • Toggle on/off from Home or Temperature tab
  • Works while using any app (Safari, IDE, etc.)
👁️

20-20-20 Eye Rest

Every 20 minutes (configurable), SYE prompts you to look away for 20 seconds.

  • Full-screen break overlay on all monitors
  • Countdown timer with Skip option
  • System notifications — macOS / Windows notification center
  • On-screen banner — 5 min, 1 min, 30 sec before break
  • Configurable interval (5 min – 2 hours) and break duration (5 sec – 10 min)
  • Active hours — auto-starts inside your schedule; manual enable works anytime
🧘

Guided Eye Exercises

Six on-demand routines with per-step timers. Longer exercises use an audio chime when each step changes.

  • 20-20-20 Focus — 20 sec
  • Figure Eight — ~1.5 min (audio)
  • Near–Far Focus — ~1.5 min (audio)
  • Blink Reset — ~45 sec (audio)
  • Palming Rest — 5 min (audio)
  • Distance Gaze — 5 min (audio)
⚡

Menu bar / system tray

Quick access without leaving your workflow.

  • Quick toggle for screen temperature
  • Eye rest on/off
  • Open SYE · Quit
🕐

Active hours

Optional schedule (e.g. 09:00–18:00). Active hours control when SYE turns features on automatically — you can always enable screen temperature or eye rest yourself from Home or the menu bar, and they stay on until you turn them off.

  • Inside the window: auto-starts if enabled in settings (e.g. computer on at 9:30 AM)
  • Outside the window: features won’t auto-start on launch
  • Manual enable always works — stays on until you disable it
  • Eye exercises are always available and ignore active hours
🚀

Launch at login

Start SYE automatically when you sign in — set it once and forget about it.

Presets

Temperature presets

Intensity controls brightness dimming only; color warmth is driven by kelvin — so you get a visible temperature shift, not just a darker screen.

Privacy

Local-first. No tracking.

  • No account required
  • No analytics SDKs
  • Settings stored on your computer
  • Screen temperature applied locally (native gamma or overlay)
  • Eye rest timers run entirely on-device

SYE Desktop does not use Supabase, Screen Time APIs, or cloud sync. The Mac and Windows app keeps all data on your machine unless cloud features are added in a future version.
